# ZedBiz Source-Tool Routing

Use the lightest available tool that reliably creates the required shot. Confirm the current runtime route and cost before naming a paid provider in a production handoff.

## Percify

Use Percify mainly when a visible presenter or avatar must speak approved words with lip movement.

Good uses:

- talking-avatar hook;
- presenter explanation or call to action;
- approved character delivering an exact narration extract;
- lip-sync-critical source footage.

Usually avoid Percify for:

- screenshots, screen recordings, logos, captions, or exact text;
- simple B-roll;
- backgrounds, product cards, or basic motion graphics;
- non-speaking shots another callable generator can create;
- final timeline assembly.

Percify may expose general image or video models, but availability does not make it the default. Check cost first. Generate only the presenter footage the edit needs, and cover suitable portions with cutaways while the approved narration continues.

## Native Video Generation

Use the runtime's callable video-generation tool for original non-speaking motion or B-roll when real media is unavailable or less useful.

Good uses:

- a frustrated customer reacting in a coffee shop;
- a visually exaggerated problem or emotional beat;
- atmospheric motion;
- an image-to-video movement based on an approved reference.

Describe one clear action, setting, camera behavior, lighting, and style. Avoid asking the model to create exact words, logos, prices, websites, or several actions in one clip.

Treat every returned clip as source material. Keep the best moment and remove weak starts, broken hands, dead holds, or unstable endings.

## Image Generation And Graphic Production

Use image generation for character references, backgrounds, objects, illustrations, or other still imagery. Use the ZedBiz graphic-production process for designed information such as branded panels, end cards, diagrams, thumbnails, and product graphics.

Use real or editable assets for exact words, prices, logos, URLs, and interface screens. Review important images with the creative-asset critique process before they enter the video.

## Real And Existing Media

Prefer real screenshots, screen recordings, owned footage, approved company assets, or properly licensed stock footage when accuracy, proof, authenticity, or speed matters.

Examples:

- show the real software screen instead of generating a fake interface;
- show the real review when discussing review quality;
- use approved character or product references instead of allowing identity drift.

## Remotion

Use Remotion to assemble and design the final timeline. It can:

- place and trim presenter footage, B-roll, screenshots, still images, and audio;
- animate exact text, images, screenshots, diagrams, and simple backgrounds;
- create crops, zooms, reframes, split screens, overlays, callouts, and transitions;
- add captions, logos, offers, URLs, calls to action, music, and effects as editable layers;
- reuse the same source clip as several shorter shots; and
- render repeatable review and final versions.

Remotion is not a realistic footage generator. It turns approved source material into the designed finished video.

## FFmpeg And FFprobe

Use FFmpeg to trim, mute, extract, convert, scale, crop, concatenate simple compatible media, and prepare assets. Use FFprobe to inspect duration, dimensions, frame rate, codecs, and audio streams.

These tools can technically process or assemble video, but Remotion is normally easier to revise when a multi-shot designed timeline matters.

## Quick Decision Order

For every shot, ask:

1. Does accurate real media already exist? Use it.
2. Does the viewer need to see the presenter speaking? Use the approved talking-avatar or lip-sync route.
3. Is it exact text, a screenshot, a diagram, or a branded layout? Build it as an editable graphic or Remotion layer.
4. Does it need original non-speaking motion? Use the available general video generator.
5. Would a still image with useful Remotion movement do the job? Avoid unnecessary video-generation cost.
6. Is it only trimming, muting, conversion, or inspection? Use FFmpeg or FFprobe.

Record the selected source route in the handoff. Production must verify that the route is callable and obtain approval for meaningful new spending before submitting a paid job.
