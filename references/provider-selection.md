# Provider And Route Selection

Choose by the approved job, not novelty. Verify live access, schema, price, terms, duration, resolution, aspect ratios, reference-image support, output ownership, and failure behavior before use.

## Route Types

- **Speaking avatar or lip-sync:** use a specialist that accepts the approved audio extract unchanged and can preserve a saved identity.
- **Generated cinematic scene or B-roll:** use a video model suited to the shot duration, motion, resolution, identity controls, and approved cost.
- **Stock or owned footage:** prefer it when realism, factual accuracy, brand fidelity, or licensing certainty matters more than synthesis.
- **Screen recording:** use a deterministic capture process for software, websites, and exact interface demonstrations.
- **Assembly:** use an editable composition system for captions, logos, overlays, repeatable revisions, and final layout.
- **Preparation and validation:** use FFmpeg or an equivalent local media tool.

## Current ZedBiz Profile

Treat this as a route preference, not proof of availability. Verify the live runtime and current price.

- Percify is the primary specialist comparison route for reusable speaking avatars and corner presenters when its live schema accepts the approved audio extract.
- Veo is the current general-purpose OpenClaw route for cinematic scenes and B-roll where the installed connector and provider access are verified.
- Grok Imagine is a cost-aware cinematic option through an agent with a verified xAI route.
- Sora is an alternative for shots or comparisons suited to the active OpenAI video route.
- HeyGen or Vidnoz are comparison or specialist routes when a project needs their presenter quality, account visibility, or manual dashboard workflow.
- Remotion is the preferred ZedBiz editable branded assembly route; FFmpeg supports preparation, repair, inspection, and delivery conversion.
- ClawVid is optional orchestration. Do not add it to a production runtime until its repository, licence, dependencies, network calls, file writes, caching, retries, outside-audio import, cost, and controlled pilot pass an isolated review.

## Paid Job Controls

- Discover the model and fetch its current schema before submission.
- Validate inputs and estimate credits or cost.
- Require approval for material paid work.
- Store the exact prompt, model, parameters, job ID, file ID when applicable, runtime, estimate, actual cost, and downloaded result.
- Poll the real job. Do not submit duplicates because a provider is slow.
- A successful model listing proves access, not output quality. Only a controlled generation proves the route, and only an end-to-end pilot proves production readiness.
