# Video Quality And Completion Gates

## Rights And Input Gate

- Script, audio, likeness, voice, footage, music, fonts, logos, and client assets have the required approvals and usage rights.
- The Master Brief, script version, audio package, Character Consistency Packages, brand inputs, destination, cost ceiling, and approval owner are resolved.

## Technical Gate

- The file opens, plays, and contains the expected video and audio streams.
- Duration, dimensions, aspect ratio, frame rate, container, video codec, audio codec, file size, and delivery encoding match the brief.
- Default web delivery is H.264 video with AAC audio unless the platform requires another format.
- Narration is present, intelligible, unclipped, and within the approved loudness target when specified.
- No unintended black, frozen, duplicated, truncated, or corrupt sections remain.

## Content Gate

- Finished-video transcription matches the approved script, allowing only documented formatting or pronunciation differences.
- Scene order, visual purpose, call to action, offers, names, numbers, URLs, and required disclosures are correct.
- Captions match the final master, are readable, synchronized, and remain inside safe areas.
- Required logos and exact text are crisp and readable rather than generated inside footage.

## Visual Gate

- Recurring people remain recognizably consistent with the approved identity package.
- Required wardrobe, product, location, and brand details are correct.
- Generated backgrounds contain no distracting distortions or mangled required text.
- Crops, overlays, transitions, pacing, colour, and visual relevance meet the brief.

## Record And Approval Gate

- Manifest identifies providers, models, prompts, parameters, job IDs, file IDs, source or licence records, costs, filenames, versions, QC, and stale assets.
- Accepted provider files and the editable project are retained outside provider dashboards.
- A human or authorized approval owner accepts the proof.
- Publication or client delivery is separately authorized when required.
- Completion reporting separates verified production results from documented or untested capability.
