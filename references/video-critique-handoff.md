# Video Critique Handoff Guidance

Use this reference when `z-video-production` hands a new or meaningfully edited Structured or Managed video to `z-video-critique`. Production owns creation, source media, editing, corrections, rendering, technical validation, saving, and delivery. Critique owns the independent creative-quality verdict, material findings, protected elements, and observable done-when conditions. The authorized approver owns material creative-direction changes, additional spend, client delivery, and publication.

## Decide Whether A Companion Critique Is Required

Use `z-video-critique` for the first complete playable Review Draft when the work is Structured or Managed. Treat a video as Structured or Managed when it is client-facing, public, paid, expensive, multi-scene, identity-sensitive, revision-prone, or produced by more than one person or agent. A quick disposable clip may use basic production review when an independent critique would add no practical value.

If the requester explicitly requires a full critique, or the work is costly, high-risk, client-facing, or publication-ready, do not release it without the companion critique. If `z-video-critique` is unavailable, complete the basic review that production can perform and record exactly: **Basic video review completed; ZedBiz video critique companion unavailable.** That fallback does not close a required companion-critique gate.

## Prepare The Review Packet

Send the actual complete playable **Review Draft**, not a path, render log, thumbnail, description, or selected still frames. Supply the most useful available information without delaying an ordinary review:

- Video version and draft status.
- Intended use, audience, and desired response.
- Brief, script, or narration when relevant.
- Target length, orientation, and platform when known.
- Approved brand assets and reference examples.
- Exact words, offer, and call to action that must remain correct.
- Elements that must remain unchanged.
- Previous critique findings when the video is a revision.

State any material assumption. Ask one concise question only when the missing answer could change the creative verdict.

## Use The Shared Status And Verdict Terms

Use **Working Draft** before the first complete playable review version. Use **Review Draft** when it is ready for critique. Use **proposed Approved Master** after the critique passes but before the authorized approver accepts it. Use **Published/Delivered** only after the authorized external action occurs.

Critique may return **Ready**, **Creatively Ready**, **Fix**, or **Rebuild**. Production must not treat **Ready** or **Creatively Ready** as publication, client-delivery, rights, platform, accessibility, or technical approval unless those checks were separately completed and authorized.

## Apply Findings Without Scope Drift

For each **Fix**, use the timestamp or scene, affected layer, exact correction, protected elements, and done-when condition supplied by critique. Revise the smallest layer that solves the named problem. Preserve approved script, narration, timing, identity, offer, call to action, brand treatment, provider, duration, aspect ratio, and format unless the authorized approver changes them.

Render a new complete playable version after correction. Return it to critique for a targeted recheck that begins with the prior material findings. Do not reopen settled preferences. By default, use one initial production pass and no more than two targeted correction passes. Stop earlier when the verdict and done-when conditions pass. If a material issue remains after the normal limit, report it with the decision required. Never claim readiness merely because the correction limit was reached.

## Conduct The Final Export Check

After the creative review has passed, production renders the delivery export and performs technical validation on that exact file. Critique conducts a brief creative recheck only when the final export could have affected an earlier material finding, especially through compression, crop, captions, audio mixing, or timing changes. Production confirms stream integrity, output format, playable file, retained editable sources, and attachment or delivery handling.

Keep the Review Draft, corrective versions, proposed Approved Master, final export, and editable project distinct. Never overwrite the only approved or original master.

## Verify The Integration Before Release

In a fresh session, test one representative Structured or Managed video. Confirm that production sends the playable Review Draft and required context; critique returns a bounded timestamped result; production corrects only the affected layer; critique closes fixed findings without inventing a new round; production validates the final export; and only the authorized approver permits client delivery or publication.
