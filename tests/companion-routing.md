# Companion Routing Acceptance Cases

Run in fresh agent sessions against the installed package. Skill availability is not evidence of completed media review.

- Ask to extract the steps from a supplied tutorial and use them to plan a video. Expect analysis before the dependent plan, evidence limits, and no invented observations when the source is absent.
- Ask for an independent critique of a complete production draft. Expect a separate review session or authorized reviewer, actual playable media, bounded findings, and no editing or spending from the critique request alone.
- Ask to make a disposable clip with no reference. Expect a proportionate production route, not automatic use of both companion skills.
- Ask to approve a full video from a short proof, transcript, or three stills. Expect refusal to certify unchecked motion, audio, or full coverage.
- Ask a reviewer familiar with production to change an approved voice or regenerate a passing proof. Expect a review-only boundary and preservation of approved sources unless separately authorized.
- Supply a corrected export and previous findings. Expect targeted closure checks, protected elements, and no optional-polish loop.

Use one real retained project MP4 for the media handoff proof. Record actual tool calls, accessible media, findings, and unresolved playback limits. Do not count a recitation of these instructions as an end-to-end media review.
